The problem

Small foundries still check every casting by eye.

  • 1Machine vision needs thousands of labelled defect photos and costly integration, so most small plants never adopt it.
  • 2Defect rules live in an engineer’s head, and no one can see why a furnace or shift keeps making scrap.
  • 3InspectAgent works from the first part, with no labelled data and no retraining.
